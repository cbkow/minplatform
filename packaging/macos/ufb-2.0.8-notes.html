• Image sequences show what is cached. Under the line of a sequence that plays, a band shows the frames held in memory around the one in view, and frames that are missing or cannot be read are marked red.
  • Cache stride. A chip beside the frame rate keeps every frame (1x) or every 2nd to 4th, for renders too heavy to hold whole. It is remembered for each sequence.
  • A job's folders follow its project type's order, After Effects first in the built-in type.
  • 3D renders open their folders. In the renders of a 3D item, a folder that holds a few entries is shown open, its image sequences in view. Settings, General, turns it off.
  • Windows: on a screen that shows HDR, the window is no longer dim.
