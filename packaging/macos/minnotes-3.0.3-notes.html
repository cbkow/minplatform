• New: rewind and fast-forward buttons on a video's strip, either side of play, in a note and on a canvas. Hold one to run through the clip, as J and L do.
  • A video's strip is easier to read: a lighter line to scrub on that runs to the end of the strip, the timecode in the players' face, and in a note the strip and a video note's words now stand on a rounded plate.
  • A video card's strip on a canvas can be narrower, so it no longer sticks out so far from a small card.
  • Windows: on a screen that shows HDR, the window is no longer dim.
