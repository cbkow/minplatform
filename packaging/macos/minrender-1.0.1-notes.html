minRender 1.0 is the same farm, rebuilt from the ground up. It works in one farm with 0.5.10 and 0.5.11 nodes, so machines can be upgraded one at a time; a node keeps its settings and its identity.

  • Edit a running job, submit part of a job as a separate job, and Farm Cleanup.
  • EXR layers in the frame preview; priority, Open output and a log picker in reach.
  • No farm is not the end — a node that starts before its shared folder is reachable waits for it and joins by itself.
  • Safer on synced folders (Dropbox, Synology Drive, LucidLink and the like) as the farm's shared folder.
  • Blender 5.3 templates and add-on.
  • After Effects and Cinema 4D templates and plug-ins are no longer included; they remain in the 0.5.x source.

Action needed — install the Blender add-on again on each workstation, from the shared folder's plugins/blender. macOS 13 or later is required. Read Upgrading from 0.5 first.
