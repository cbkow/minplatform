• New: a pen tool for paths. Click for corners, drag for curves, click the first point to close; Enter, Escape or a right click ends a path. It is in the drawing tools of videos, pictures, PDF pages and canvases.
  • New: four path tools beside it: select points (with a box over empty ground), add a point, remove a point, and turn a corner into a curve or back.
  • New: the arrow selects several drawings at once, with a box or with Shift. They move, are deleted and take a colour or a width together.
  • A video note's text now stands in a bar under the video's controls, on two lines; point at it to read all of it. The picture stays clear. The same on a selected video card of a canvas.
  • A clip stored on its side (a phone's) is shown upright: in the note, while it plays and in a web folder.
  • QCView's "Export to minNotes" and "Open in minNotes" are taken: a note is made of the clip, or of a stream's notes.
  • Fixed: a collection could not be saved on LucidLink.
  • Fixed: freehand lines drawn fast could run past where the pen stopped, or look different in an export.
